# 群峦精妙背包升级
## 添加了群峦版本的背包及其升级插件

### 模组内容
1. 添加了六种背包：铋铜背包、青铜背包、黑铜背包对应精妙背包的铁制背包；锻铁背包对应精妙背包的金制背包；钢制背包对应精妙背包的钻石背包；黑钢对应精妙背包的下界合金背包。
2. 添加了各金属等级的堆叠升级。
3. 添加了冰箱升级插件，用于在背包保存食物，延长其保质期。它内带一个电源开关，在玩家背的背包内有电池的情况下，会消耗电池电量更好的延长食物保质期。
4. 添加了烤炉升级插件，用于在背包内烹饪食物，兼容群峦的加热配方以及FirmaLife的烤炉配方。
5. 添加了坩埚升级插件，用于在背包内加热或冶炼金属。该升级插件内有放风箱的位置，通过点击按钮触发风箱，可以额外提升燃料温度。它内带一个电源开关，在玩家背的背包内有电池的情况下，会消耗电池电量提升温度，并且可以锁定在一定的温度值。
6. 添加了锻打升级插件，用于在背包内锻造物品，默认为铜砧，可以在插件内放置更高等级的金属砧设置其等级。

# TFC Sophisticated Backpacks Upgrade
## Added backpack and upgrade for TFC

### Mod Content
1. Six types of backpacks have been added: Bismuth Bronze Backpack, Bronze Backpack, and Black Bronze Backpack correspond to the Iron Backpack; Wrought Iron Backpack corresponds to the Gold Backpack; Steel Backpack corresponds to the Diamond Backpack; Black Steel corresponds to the Netherite Backpack.
2. Added various metal tiers of Stack upgrades. 
3. Added fridge upgrade to preserve food in the backpack and extend its shelf life. It comes with an internal power switch. When there are batteries in the player's backpack, it will consume the battery power to better extend the shelf life of food.
4. Added oven upgrade to cook food inside the backpack, compatible with the TFC heating recipes and FirmaLife oven recipes.
5. Added crucible upgrade, used for heating or smelting metals in the backpack. The upgrade plugin includes a slot for a bellows. By clicking the button to activate the bellows, the fuel temperature can be further increased. It comes with a power switch. When there are batteries in the player's backpack, it will consume battery power to increase temperature and can be locked at a certain temperature value.
6. Added forging upgrade, used for forging items in the backpack. The default is a copper anvil, but you can place a higher-level metal anvil within the plugin and set its level.
